Fine print: delivery, retakes, open book, renewal
  • Price is regional: USD 165 in the United States (discounts for students and some programs).
  • Pearson VUE test center or online proctored (OnVUE): clean desk, webcam, ID, no second monitor.
  • Non-native speakers can request +30 minutes if the exam isn't offered in their language.
  • Open book: Microsoft Learn is available in a split screen, but the timer keeps running. Use it only to confirm one fact.
  • Multi-part items may give partial credit; some unscored "beta" items may appear.
  • Mostly GA features; commonly used preview features can appear.
  • Retakes: 24 hours after the first fail, then 14 days; max 5 attempts in 12 months.
  • Renewal: free, unproctored assessment on Learn; window opens 180 days before expiry; retries allowed.
  • Free Practice Assessment and an exam sandbox (demo of the exam UI) exist.
  • Some exams include lab-style tasks; hands-on practice in a Fabric trial is the only preparation.
  • Length, item count and question types vary between exam forms: treat the numbers as typical.

  • 700, minus points for wrong answers

What changed compared with older outlines
  • 2024 launch outline (until mid-Nov 2024): 4 domains: Plan/implement/manage (10–15 %), Prepare and serve data (40–45 %), Semantic models (20–25 %), Explore and analyze (20–25 %). New: security (RLS/CLS/OLS/file-level, DDM, labels, endorsement), database projects, eventhouse/KQL, Real-Time hub, OneLake integration, Direct Lake fallback/refresh. Removed: report themes, predictive visuals, report authoring.
  • 2026-04-20 revision: same domains and weights, current names: OneLake catalog, OneLake security, DAX queries, Direct Lake on OneLake vs on SQL endpoint, no automatic default semantic models, Activator, Copy job, Fabric data agent. Datamarts are being retired (use a warehouse).
  • Platform knowledge (capacities, licensing, OneLake) is assumed everywhere: see 01 Fabric platform. Lakehouse/Delta/Spark mechanics underpin many Prepare data questions: 05.

Exam-day tactics

Underline the qualifier: it decides between two plausible answers.

"Least privilege"
Item permission over workspace role; Viewer over Contributor.
"Without copying data"
Shortcuts, mirroring, Direct Lake.
"Minimize development effort"
Copy job or Dataflow Gen2, not notebooks.
"T-SQL writes / multi-table transactions"
Warehouse (lakehouse SQL endpoint is read-only).
"Streaming logs, time series, KQL"
Eventhouse. Spark or files → lakehouse.
"Users must not query the source"
Fixed-identity model + model RLS.
"Free-license users must view reports"
F64+ (or P SKU) + Viewer or app access.
